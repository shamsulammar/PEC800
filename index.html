WELLNESS MANAGEMENT

“8 Dimensions of Wellness”
30 Students • 8 Groups • 8 Dimensions • 1 Class Wellness Experience

Eight dimensions. One balanced you.

Wellness is more than being physically healthy. Our overall well-being is influenced by different areas of our lives: physical, emotional, spiritual, intellectual, occupational, environmental, financial, and social.

Today's class will involve:

  • Learning about the 8 dimensions of wellness
  • Forming 8 student groups
  • Designing interactive classroom activities
  • Leading one activity each week over 7 weeks
  • Learning from other groups
  • Working toward a final class wellness project

Group Mission

YOUR GROUP MISSION

Each group becomes the Wellness Team for ONE dimension.

  1. Understand the assigned wellness dimension.
  2. Discuss how it affects everyday life.
  3. Design ONE interactive classroom activity.
  4. Make the activity suitable for the entire class.
  5. Lead the activity.
  6. Encourage participation.
  7. Explain what the class should learn from the activity.
  8. Reflect after completing the activity.

Requirements

CLASSROOM ACTIVITY REQUIREMENTS

Each group creates ONE 5 to 10 minute activity. One group leads its activity each week of the 7-week semester. The Physical group is different: it prepares the final project (Euro Dance) over more than one week.

Avoid

Long lecturesExpensive equipmentOnly one or two students participating

Teacher notes: activity timing

  • Suggested run of show: 5 to 10 minutes per group x 8 groups = 40 to 80 minutes, plus 1 to 2 minutes of transition between groups.
  • If time is tight, run 4 groups per session and keep the strict 5 minute version.
  • Ask groups to bring a backup plan in case the room or equipment is limited.

Special Final Project Role

PHYSICAL GROUP: EURO DANCE TEAM

The group assigned to PHYSICAL wellness will have an additional responsibility for the final project. Because a Euro Dance needs time to design, teach and rehearse, this group prepares over more than one week instead of conducting a single weekly activity.

  1. Coordinate the Euro Dance choreography.
  2. Select/design a suitable dance routine.
  3. Create simple choreography that everyone can follow.
  4. Teach the choreography to the class.
  5. Break the choreography into manageable sections.
  6. Plan formations and transitions.
  7. Organize practice sessions.
  8. Coordinate with the other groups.
  9. Lead the final Euro Dance performance.

The objective is PARTICIPATION, not professional dancing.

Everyone should have an opportunity to participate.

PHYSICAL TEAM= MOVEMENT TEAM= EURO DANCE TEAM

Teacher notes: Physical group / Euro Dance

  • Confirm the Physical group understands the extra workload before finalising the assignment.
  • Check safety: space in the room, footwear, and moves that suit all fitness levels. Offer seated or low-impact options.
  • Agree a practice schedule and a checkpoint date to review the choreography plan and music selection.
  • Other groups should nominate a contact person to coordinate with the Physical group.
